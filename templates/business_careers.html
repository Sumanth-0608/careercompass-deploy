Key Business, Finance & Strategy Pathways

Accounting & Law Statutory ICAI License

Chartered Accountant (CA) & Auditor

Statutory financial auditing, direct and indirect taxation, corporate compliance, and strategic financial management.

Prerequisite: ICAI Foundation / Direct Entry + Intermediate + 2-Yr Articleship + Final
Core Skills: Tax Laws, Audit Vouching, Ind AS, Financial Statements
Trade-off: Rigorous pass rates requiring sustained exam discipline

Data & Operations High Corporate Demand

Business Analyst

Bridges business needs with technology solutions: analyzes workflows, writes user stories, models financial feasibility, and translates stakeholder goals.

Education: B.Com, BBA, Economics, Engineering, or Data Analytics
Core Skills: SQL, Excel Financial Models, Process Mapping, Jira
Beginner Project: Audit a local business's checkout queue & model savings
Reality Check → Roadmap
Tech Strategy Cross-Functional

Product Manager

Aligns engineering, UX design, and commercial strategy to define product roadmaps, prioritize feature sprints, and solve customer pain points.

Education: Engineering, Commerce, or Design; often MBA transition
Core Skills: User Discovery, PRDs, Wireframing (Figma), Funnel Analytics
Trade-off: Full accountability with no direct authority over team
Reality Check → Roadmap
Brand & Growth Creative & Analytical

Marketing Specialist & Brand Strategist

Designs multichannel customer acquisition funnels, content strategies, brand storytelling, and data-driven ad campaigns.

Education: BBA, Mass Comm, Commerce, or proven digital portfolio
Core Skills: SEO, Content Copywriting, GA4 Analytics, Meta Ads
Beginner Project: Run a campus club newsletter and track open rates
Reality Check → Roadmap
Logistics & Scale Essential Commerce

Operations & Supply Chain Manager

Optimizes end-to-end material procurement, warehouse inventory buffers, freight dispatch, and lean factory workflows.

Education: Engineering, Commerce, BBA in Supply Chain / MBA
Core Skills: Demand Forecasting, SAP/ERP, Vendor Contracting, Lean
Sector Demand: E-commerce, Quick Commerce, Manufacturing, Exports
Reality Check → Roadmap
People & Culture Corporate Governance

Human Resources (HR) & Talent Specialist

Leads candidate recruitment, onboarding, statutory workplace compliance, performance appraisals, and conflict mediation.

Education: BBA, Psychology, Commerce, followed by MBA in HR / SHRM
Core Skills: Competency Interviews, Labour Laws, HRIS, Mediation
Key Impact: Fostering safe, fair, and high-performing workplace teams
Reality Check → Roadmap
Venture Creation High Autonomy

Entrepreneur / Startup Founder

Identifies unaddressed market problems, builds initial minimum viable products (MVPs), secures early customers, and manages capital risk.

Reality Check: High financial ambiguity; requires rapid testing and frugal budgeting
Core Skills: Sales, Cash Flow Discipline, Resilience, Team Leadership
