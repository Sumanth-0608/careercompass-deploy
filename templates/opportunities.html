📄

CV & Resume Guidance

For student applications, proof of curiosity outweighs years of experience.

  • Highlight tangible projects (AutoCAD drawings, GitHub repositories, school event budgets).
  • Keep it to exactly 1 page in a clean, single-column PDF format.
  • Include relevant coursework, software tools, and verifiable certifications (NPTEL, Google, Coursera).
  • Proofread carefully; zero spelling errors creates an immediate positive impression.
🎯

Interview Preparation

Structure your answers using the STAR Method (Situation, Task, Action, Result).

  • Prepare a 60-second summary answering "Tell me about yourself and your career interest."
  • Be ready to explain every project listed on your resume in depth.
  • Admit honestly when you don't know a technical concept, but explain how you would research it.
  • Prepare 2 thoughtful questions for the interviewer about day-to-day team challenges.
📁

Portfolio Suggestions

Tailor your demonstration artifact according to your target field:

  • Civil Engineering: PDF portfolio with 2 floor plans, structural calculations, and site visit photos.
  • Software & IT: Active GitHub with 2 deployed web apps and clean README documentation.
  • Business & Finance: Spreadsheet models, process audit reports, or market research decks.
  • Sports & Athletics: Tournament bracket schedules, video tagging clips, or first-aid training certificates.
✉️

Professional Communication

Write respectful, structured inquiry messages that respect mentors' time.

Subject: Student Application: [Role] – [Your Name] (B.Tech / Class)
Dear [Name/Hiring Team],
I am a student at [College] interested in [Domain]. I completed [Project] and would love to contribute to [Team]...
Always follow up politely after 7–10 business days if no reply is received.
🛡️

Scam-Awareness Checklist

Protect yourself from fraudulent employment and fake internship offers:

  • NEVER pay money for application fees, security deposits, training kits, or job guarantees. Legitimate employers pay you.
  • Verify official domains (e.g. .gov.in for government portals, corporate email instead of generic Gmail/WhatsApp).
  • Be cautious of unsolicited job offers on Telegram or WhatsApp promising unrealistic high pay for clicking links.
  • Verify the organization on the official MCA (Ministry of Corporate Affairs) portal or university training cell.
