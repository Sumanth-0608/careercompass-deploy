Science & Health Statutory BPT Required

Sports Physiotherapist

Specializes in on-field emergency care, musculoskeletal rehabilitation, biomechanical assessment, and return-to-play protocols for athletes.

Core Degree: Bachelor of Physiotherapy (BPT, 4.5 yrs) + MPT
Mandatory Council: NCAHP / State Physiotherapy Council
Key Skills: Taping, Manual Therapy, Biomechanics, CPR

Sports Analyst & Performance Specialist

Tags match footage, processes GPS tracking and heart-rate telemetry, and builds tactical scouting reports for coaching teams.

Education: Sports Science, Data Analytics, Statistics, or CS
Tools: Python, Tableau, Kinovea, Video Tagging (Hudl)
Beginner Project: Opposition video breakdown using open data

Athletic Coach & Sports Trainer

Guides youth and elite athletes in skill drills, tactical awareness, team strategy, and mental resilience across sports academies and schools.

Education: B.P.Ed / NSNIS Diploma in Coaching + Federation Licenses
Institutes: NIS Patiala, LNIPE Gwalior, State Sports Councils
Key Skills: Drill Design, Motivation, Game Psychology

Human Physiology B.Sc / M.Sc Track

Sports Science Specialist & Physiologist

Operates high-performance laboratory equipment (force plates, metabolic analyzers) to track VO2 max, fatigue markers, and power output.

Education: B.Sc / M.Sc in Sports Science, Exercise Physiology
Institutes: National Sports University (NSU), SAI High Performance Labs
Key Skills: Biomechanics, VO2 Max Testing, Periodization

Education: Bachelor of Physical Education (B.P.Ed) / M.P.Ed
